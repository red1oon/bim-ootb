Load Summary

What your drawings tell the structural engineer. Not a design check.
This page weighs what the drawings show, using the unit weights of BNBC 2012 Part 6 Chapter 2 (Tables 2.2.1 / 2.2.2; the gazetted BNBC 2020 text was not checked). It does not compute live load, wind, earthquake or member strength: those need the inputs listed in red below and an engineer's analysis.
