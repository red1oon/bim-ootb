        What to know

        Limits, in plain words
        • The history is kept in a hidden sheet of the workbook named _door_history. Excel, WPS and the like carry it along when they save; if someone deletes that sheet the history is gone, and this page cannot tell that it ever existed.
        • If someone edits the history by hand, this page notices and names the entry.
        • Changes made between two stamps are recorded as one step.
        • The file you download keeps values and formulas. Cell styles (colours, widths, borders) are not kept by this page: stamp a copy if the look matters.
        • A name is whatever was typed. It is not proof of who made a change.
