## §DOC-SAVE - a generic twin divergence found here (AD-LAYER LAW: fixed in the engine)
The twin's `runWorkflow` wrote DocStatus/Processed/DocAction with `trx.update`, so NO AFTER_CHANGE validator ever saw a completion (MFG_Validator's C_Order hook could never fire). Now `runWorkflow` snapshots the row before the D-node and, after `processIt`, saves the difference through `save(trx, table, before, changes)`; a refused save aborts the node ("SaveError"), as legacy.
