## Why this framing (measured 2026-10-22, advice given to the owner)
"Misaligned layers" is not the right word: inside one sheet the layers agree. What a 2D set lacks is the link BETWEEN sheets: each sheet is its own flat world, and what joins them (which face, which way, which height, which storey, where the cut, which unit) is written only for a person (titles, level texts, conventions). Measured: on our own Duplex the plans share a frame but every elevation has height on y, two run backwards, the left one runs 0..18.55 m against a plan depth of -21.1..3.3 m; units are in the header. On the shed (BricsCAD, inches) the sheets sit apart in model space (floor plan grid 01 at x -26,808, beam layout -21,514, foundation -19,438 drawing units), the elevations' ground lines at y -4,470.6 / -3,919.2 / -4,539.7 / -3,790.4, North runs -X, West and East -Y: a stronger picture, so the film uses the shed.
