## Facts it stands on (measured 2026-10-22)
- `modeller/dxf_import_ui.js` `buildCanvas` places each sheet with one function `w(h, v)` per role: plan / roof `[x*k, y*k, level | roofZ]`; elevation `toPlan(h)` along the axis of `Dxf2Marks.align(plans[0].desc, sheet.desc)`, `zOf(v)` up, on the face plane of the lowest plan's wall envelope; section the same with the `@CUT` plane. A sheet with no role, or one that does not align, is NOT drawn (the plain Duplex set shows an empty canvas).
- Plain Duplex (our writer, mm): the plans share one frame (x -3.3..12.1 m, y -21.1..3.3 m); every elevation has height on y (-1.2..7.06 m); REAR (x -9.4..0.75) and RIGHT (x -18.4..0.75) run backwards; LEFT runs 0..18.55 m against a plan depth of -21.1..3.3 m.

## Behaviour
1. **Button `Arrange`** in the 2D Drawings panel footer (beside Lines / Metadata; a Lucide line icon from the panel's icon set, as the other buttons). Enabled when the set is open. Click = play; click again or Esc = stop at the final placement (the normal canvas). API for scripts: `DxfImportUI.arrange({ stepMs, holdMs })` returns a Promise resolved at the end; `DxfImportUI.arrangeStop()`.
2. **Stage 0, as drawn.** Every sheet of the set (placed or not) is drawn FLAT at its raw drawing coordinates, 1 drawing unit = 1 mm, z = 0, in its role colour (no role = grey), bright layers as the canvas does. The rule "1 unit = 1 mm before @UNITS" is a stated starting point of the view, not a reading; the panel / status line says so. The camera frames the whole pile and holds (`holdMs`).
3. **Steps.** Sheet by sheet (plans by level, then roof, elevations, section; sheets that cannot be placed last), one step per marking, each an eased tween of `stepMs` from the previous stage to the next. A stage is computed ONLY from the markings applied so far; the last stage of a sheet IS its `w(h, v)` (same numbers as `buildCanvas`):
   - every sheet: `UNITS` (scale about the sheet's own origin from 1 mm to the factor k; for mm a visible no-op stated as "mm: unchanged");
   - plan / roof: `LEVEL` (or `ROOF` for a roof without level) lifts to z; a plan other than the reference plan is moved onto it by the alignment the pipeline already uses (if there is none, it stays where drawn and the step says why);
   - elevation / section: `VIEW` stands it up (rotation about its own horizontal axis: y becomes height); `Z` (the level datums) moves its heights onto the stated levels (`zOf`); `HAXIS` + `GRIDH` lay its horizontal axis along the plan axis, in the stated direction, on the plan grid (`toPlan`; a turn to Y is a rotation about Z, a minus is a mirror, shown as such); `FACE` (elevation) moves it onto its face plane; `CUT` (section) onto its cut plane.
   - a sheet that cannot be placed (no role, `align` errors) stays flat and dim, with `§DXF-ARRANGE skip sheet=<name> why=<reason>`.
4. **Panel focus.** At each step `DxfMetaEditor.focusField(sheetIndex, key)`: every section but Sheets collapsed, every other sheet card closed, this sheet's card open, the panel scrolled so the field (or, for `Z` / `GRIDH`, its list rows) is in view, the field outlined in yellow for the step. During the replay the 2D Drawings panel is minimised to its header to free the canvas; the canvas dims the other sheets. Everything (sections, cards, scroll, panel) is restored at the end or on stop.
5. **Logs:** `§DXF-ARRANGE start sheets=<n> steps=<n>`, per step `§DXF-ARRANGE step <i> sheet=<name> key=<KEY> value=<value>`, `§DXF-ARRANGE skip ...`, `§DXF-ARRANGE done steps=<n> ms=<n> final_equal=<true|false>`.
6. **Not changed:** markings, verdict, gate, Convert, Save, `buildCanvas` placement. The Duplex standard set hash (writeStdSet + deriveMarks) unchanged.
