## Facts it stands on (measured 2026-10-22)

A sheet with no role, or one that does not align, is NOT drawn (the plain Duplex set shows an empty canvas).
- Plain Duplex (our writer, mm): the plans share one frame (x -3.3..12.1 m, y -21.1..3.3 m); every elevation has height on y (-1.2..7.06 m); REAR (x -9.4..0.75) and RIGHT (x -18.4..0.75) run backwards; LEFT runs 0..18.55 m against a plan depth of -21.1..3.3 m.

- Suggestions come in rounds (`modeller/dxf2suggest.js` reads each sheet's markings + the other sheets): round 1 UNITS / VIEW / NAME (+ SET when another sheet states one); round 2 needs the role: GRIDX / GRIDY (plan), GRIDH / Z / FACE (elevation), GRIDH / Z / CUT (section); HAXIS needs a plan with `@LEVEL`. No rule suggests `@LEVEL` or `@SET` from nothing. Measured on the raw shed (9 sheets): round 1 = 25 rows, round 2 = 46 bands in all, round 3 = nothing (HAXIS refused: "no plan with @LEVEL to fit against"); every elevation and the section get ONE datum (PR-Z-GROUND, the ground line), so the gate refuses them "missing Z(>=2)".
- `dxf2suggest.create(ctx)` takes its sheets from `ctx.sheets()` and reads only `sheet.marks / dxf / name / text`: a second suggester over shadow copies computes the next round as if the earlier yellow values were in place, with NO engine change (measured: the round-1 rows of the shadow = the editor's own `sh.prop.list`).
- Convert already aligns plans and places faces without walls (structure sets): `Dxf2Bodies` moves every plan after the base onto it by their shared GRIDX / GRIDY labels (all shared labels agree within 0.001 m, else that plan is refused with the reason); `Dxf2Panels` puts an elevation's panels on the END GRID LINE of the base plan on the face's side (min / max of its grid values; the yellow applied default `panel.plane = end grid line, centred`). Both rules are now one function each (`Dxf2Bodies.planOffset`, `Dxf2Panels.facePlane`), called by Convert, `buildCanvas` and Arrange.

## Behaviour
1. **Button `Arrange`** in the 2D Drawings panel footer (beside Lines / Metadata; Lucide `play`, `square` while playing). Click = play; click again or Esc = stop at the final placement. API for scripts: `DxfImportUI.arrange({ stepMs, holdMs, onStep })` returns a Promise resolved at the end (`{ steps, total, ms, final_equal, compared, worst }`); `DxfImportUI.arrangeStop()`.
2. **Play on open.** Opening a set from the chooser plays it (`DxfImportUI.state.arrangeOnOpen`, options `state.arrangeOpts`). An automated browser (`navigator.webdriver`: the existing witnesses) opens without it unless the script sets `arrangeOnOpen = true`, so no existing witness changes its steps.
   **§D38 (owner 2026-10-22: the morph is for RAW 2Ds; once processed and the metadata is in the DXF it does not run again; it gives the wow and a visual memory of before / after):** play on open only when at least one step is yellow (a defaulted value). A set whose placing values are all stated opens at its final placement and logs `§DXF-ARRANGE skip-on-open`. The `Arrange` button still replays on request. Witness A9a (marked shed: skip=1, start=0) / A9b (raw shed: start yellow=46, skip=0); a witness with no material prints SKIP, never PASS.
3. **Source = the EFFECTIVE markings** of each sheet: its file / accepted markings + its yellow suggestions (`DxfMetaEditor.preview()`): rounds computed as if the earlier yellow values were in place (still yellow, never accepted, never written into the editor's sheets). The verdict / gate / Convert keep reading the accepted markings only.
4. **Stage 0, as drawn.** Every sheet (placed or not) flat at its raw drawing coordinates, 1 drawing unit = 1 mm, z = 0, in its role colour (no role = grey), bright layers as the canvas does. "1 unit = 1 mm before @UNITS" is the stated starting point of the view, not a reading; the status line says so. The camera frames the pile (1st..99th percentile of the points) in the canvas strip the panels leave free, and holds (`holdMs`); during a step it follows the moving sheet.
5. **Steps.** Sheet by sheet (plans by level, roof, elevations, section; sheets without a role last). One step per accepted placing marking (UNITS, VIEW, LEVEL, GRIDX, GRIDY for a plan; UNITS, VIEW, ROOF for a roof plan; UNITS, VIEW, Z, HAXIS, GRIDH, FACE / CUT for an elevation / section: the keys the sheet's role needs) and one step per YELLOW BAND (all yellow rows of one key on one sheet, e.g. the 14 GRIDH rows of an elevation appear together), merged in that key order. Each step is an eased tween of `stepMs` (`stepMs * 0.4` when it moves nothing) from the previous stage to the next; a stage is computed ONLY from the values applied so far; the last stage of a sheet the canvas places IS its `w(h, v)` (`placeOf`, shared with `buildCanvas`: the same function, not a copy):
   - `UNITS` scales about the sheet's own origin from 1 mm to k ("mm: unchanged" for mm);
   - `VIEW` stands an elevation / section up (a turn about its own horizontal axis: y becomes height); a plan lies flat ("unchanged");
   - `LEVEL` (plan) / `ROOF` (roof plan) lifts to z;
   - `Z` moves the heights onto the stated datum (`zOf`, the lowest datum and the unit factor); **preview rule:** one datum + known units is enough here (vertical = z0 + (y - y0) * k), drawn yellow and listed as "one height: a second is needed to convert"; the gate rule (Z >= 2) is NOT changed;
   - `HAXIS` turns the sheet about the vertical through its own centre (where it stands) to the plan axis (Y = a quarter turn) and mirrors a minus (scale through zero, shown as such); `GRIDH` moves it onto the plan grid (`toPlan`); `FACE` onto its face plane; `CUT` onto its cut plane;
   - `GRIDX` / `GRIDY` of a plan after the base move it onto the base plan by `Dxf2Bodies.planOffset` (x, then y; logged `morph=move dx=` / `dy=`); when the labels disagree it stays where drawn and the step / note says why; the base plan's grid moves nothing ("the grid the elevations and the other plans align to"). A plan is aligned in every set (the Duplex plans share a frame: dx = dy = 0); for a wall set whose plans would move the note says that its Convert does not move plans;
   - `FACE` without a wall envelope uses the panel.plane default (`Dxf2Panels.facePlane`: the end grid line of the base plan on that side): the move is logged `default=panel.plane (the end grid line <m> m, no wall envelope)` and the sheet is tinted as a default (also on the canvas); with a wall envelope today's rule (its face). A section relies on its `@CUT`, never on the envelope;
   - NAME, SET move nothing ("names the sheet");
   - a step that cannot move the sheet says why (`needs @HAXIS (no plan with @LEVEL to fit against)`, `no face plane: no wall envelope and no grid on that side of the base plan`), and the sheet stands as far as its values allow.
6. **Yellow tint.** A sheet placed by any yellow value (a yellow step that moves it) turns yellow-tinted as that step plays ("placed by defaults, not yours yet"). After play the view stays LIVE while any sheet is tinted or stands where the canvas would not draw it; otherwise it hands over to the normal canvas at once. Live: accepting / typing a value (also during play: it is synced when the play ends) re-judges as today; the view then turns that sheet's tint solid (role colour) with no motion, or, when a value changed, tweens from where the sheet stands to the new place. Esc (not playing) hands over to the normal canvas. The 2D Drawings panel lists under each sheet what its effective values do not give ("Arrange: ...").
7. **Panel focus.** At each step `DxfMetaEditor.focusField(sheetIndex, key, yellow)`: every section but Sheets collapsed (+ Units for an accepted `@UNITS`, its field lives there), every other sheet card closed, this card open, the field / its rows (Z, GRIDH) / the yellow band in view and outlined yellow. While the view stands, a card lists every yellow value placing it ("Defaults placing this sheet (yellow: not yours yet)"; the rows of the current round keep their tick, later rounds say "after the above") in place of its suggestion block; during play the bands appear one at a time. The 2D Drawings panel is minimised to its header + footer (the sheet list hidden; the Stop button and the status line, which narrates each step, stay). Sections, cards, scroll and panel are restored at the end or on stop.
8. **Logs:** `§DXF-ARRANGE start sheets=<n> steps=<n> yellow=<n> stage0="..."`, `§DXF-ARRANGE finding sheets without a role=<n> (...)`, `§DXF-ARRANGE note sheet=<plan> moves onto <base> by its shared grid labels (...): dx= dy= m` | `... stays where drawn: <reason>`, per step `§DXF-ARRANGE step <i> sheet=<name> key=<KEY> value=<v> src=<file|user|yellow> morph=<scale|stand|lift|turn|mirror|turnmirror|move|none why=...>`, then per sheet `§DXF-ARRANGE stands sheet=<name> placed=<canvas|full-preview|partial> [tint=yellow] lacks=<...>` or `§DXF-ARRANGE skip sheet=<name> why=no role (...)`, `§DXF-ARRANGE done steps=<n>/<n> ms=<n> final_equal=<bool> compared=<n> worst=<m> tinted=<n>`, `§DXF-ARRANGE sync moved=<n> (<sheets>) tween=700ms tinted=<n>`, `§DXF-ARRANGE hand-over to the canvas (...)`.
9. **Not changed:** markings, verdict, gate, Convert, Save, the suggestion engine (`dxf2suggest.js`), the Duplex standard set writer, `buildCanvas` output for a set whose plans share a frame and whose lowest plan has walls (Duplex: identical). **Changed on purpose:** `buildCanvas` now shows a set where Convert puts it: a plan after the base moved by `planOffset`, an elevation without a wall envelope on its panel.plane default (tinted) instead of not drawn (the shed marked set: 4 sheets drawn before, 8 now, one shed).
